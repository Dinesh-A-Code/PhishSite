Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

PhishSite ("PhishSite", "we", "us", or "our") is a browser-based email security tool designed to analyze email messages and identify potentially suspicious or phishing content.

This Privacy Policy explains how PhishSite handles information when you use the application, including when you connect a Google Gmail account.

1. Information We Access

PhishSite can operate without connecting a Gmail account. If you choose to connect Gmail, the application requests read-only access to your Gmail mailbox through Google's Gmail API.

The application may access:

  • Your Google account email address for displaying the connected account.
  • Basic Gmail account/profile information provided through the Gmail API.
  • Inbox message metadata such as message IDs, subjects, senders, dates, and snippets.
  • Email headers and message content when required for security analysis.
  • Information necessary to analyze links, attachments, sender information, and authentication-related email headers.

2. How We Use Gmail Data

Gmail data is accessed only to provide the email security and phishing-analysis features of PhishSite.

This may include:

  • Displaying messages from your Gmail inbox.
  • Analyzing email content for suspicious characteristics.
  • Inspecting sender and authentication information.
  • Analyzing links and attachment information contained in messages.
  • Displaying analysis results and scan history during your session.
PhishSite does not ask you to enter your Google password into the application. Google handles authentication and authorization through Google's authentication system.

3. Google Account Authorization

When you select "Continue with Google", Google handles the authentication and authorization process.

PhishSite requests the Gmail read-only permission (gmail.readonly). This permission allows the application to read Gmail messages but does not give PhishSite permission to modify, send, delete, or otherwise change your Gmail messages.

Google may display its own authorization and privacy information during the sign-in process. Google's handling of your information is governed by Google's applicable privacy policies.

4. Where Processing Occurs

PhishSite is designed as a browser-based application. Gmail messages retrieved for analysis are processed by the application running in your browser.

The current application does not require you to provide your Gmail password to PhishSite.

5. Access Tokens

Google provides PhishSite with a temporary access token after you authorize Gmail access.

The application uses this token to communicate with Google's Gmail API during the connected session. The token is held in application memory and is not intentionally stored in browser local storage or session storage.

PhishSite also attempts to renew an expiring Gmail access token while the connected session remains active.

6. Data Retention

PhishSite's current browser implementation keeps Gmail-related session information in memory while the application is being used.

Scan history created during the session is maintained by the application for displaying results and analytics during that session.

PhishSite does not intentionally store Gmail messages or Gmail access tokens in persistent browser storage.

7. Disconnecting Gmail

You can disconnect your Gmail account from PhishSite through the application's Settings area.

When you disconnect Gmail, PhishSite revokes the current Gmail access token and clears the application's active Gmail connection state.

The application also provides an option to remove Gmail-derived scan information from the current session. Manually entered scan history is not removed by that Gmail-specific cleanup.

8. Information You Enter Manually

PhishSite may allow you to manually enter email content for analysis. Such information is processed to provide the requested security analysis and may appear in the application's current-session scan history.

9. Security

PhishSite uses security measures appropriate to its browser-based architecture, including Google's OAuth authentication flow, read-only Gmail permissions, access-token lifecycle handling, and a Content Security Policy.

No method of transmitting or processing information over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should therefore avoid submitting information that they do not want analyzed.

10. Third-Party Services

PhishSite uses Google services to authenticate users and access Gmail through the Gmail API.

Google processes information according to Google's own policies and terms. PhishSite does not control Google's independent processing of information.

11. Children's Privacy

PhishSite is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the application.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when PhishSite's features, data-handling practices, or legal requirements change.

The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was most recently revised.
